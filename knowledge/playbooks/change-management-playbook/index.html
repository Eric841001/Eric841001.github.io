변화 관리 플레이북

Executive Summary​

Technology deployment does not guarantee business adoption.

Plan leadership responsibilities, communication, training, support and feedback alongside the technical rollout.

This playbook provides a framework for driving user adoption and realizing business value from Microsoft 365, Copilot, Security and Digital Workplace initiatives.

Microsoft 365 Change Management

Move from deployment to measurable adoption

Successful adoption connects leadership alignment, role-based communication, education, champion networks, office hours, support and continuous improvement.
LeadershipEducationChampionsAnalytics
Adoption Operating ModelBusiness change to sustained usage
01AlignSet sponsorship, steering committee, business leader and champion responsibilities.02CommunicateExplain why the change matters and what each audience should do next.03EnableDeliver role-based learning, prompt clinics, use case workshops and office hours.04MeasureUse usage, VOC, ticket, training and business outcome signals to improve.
Adoption ruleDo not measure success by license assignment alone. Measure behavior change, confidence, support demand and business outcomes.

Change Management Framework​

Change Management FrameworkLeadership signal to adoption improvement loop
01LeadershipConfirm why change is needed, expected outcome and sponsorship behavior.
02CommunicationTailor message, timing, audience and channel by adoption phase.
03EducationUse role-based learning, hands-on practice and prompt coaching.
04ChampionsSupport departments, collect feedback and reinforce practical use cases.
05ImproveUse adoption analytics, VOC, blockers and value signals to refresh the program.

Objectives​

The program should achieve:

  • User awareness
  • User engagement
  • Behavioral change
  • Productivity improvement
  • Business outcome realization
  • Sustainable adoption

Executive Sponsorship​

Assign an executive sponsor to communicate the business purpose, support adoption and resolve decisions that cross teams. See Microsoft Teams adoption roles.

Key activities:

  • Executive announcements
  • Leadership communication
  • Adoption KPI review
  • Success story sharing
  • Department accountability

Communication Strategy​

Phase 1 – Awareness​

Objectives:

  • Explain why change is required
  • Communicate business value
  • Set expectations

Channels:

  • Town Hall
  • Leadership email
  • Intranet
  • Video messages

Phase 2 – Adoption​

Objectives:

  • Encourage usage
  • Promote success stories
  • Increase confidence

Channels:

  • Weekly newsletters
  • Use case campaigns
  • Community channels
  • Department briefings

Phase 3 – Optimization​

Objectives:

  • Reinforce behaviors
  • Expand advanced use cases
  • Improve business outcomes

Channels:

  • Monthly updates
  • Executive dashboards
  • Champion meetings

Continuous Improvement Model​

Continuous Improvement ModelLearning, usage and feedback refresh the adoption system
01EducationRole-based learning, scenario practice and prompt coaching.
02UsageReal work scenarios in meetings, documents, analysis and collaboration.
03FeedbackVOC, ticket trends, champion input and office-hour questions.
04InsightsPatterns, blockers, confidence gaps and use case opportunities.
05OptimizationRefresh content, support model, governance messages and next wave plan.

Lessons Learned​

  • Technology deployment is only the beginning
  • Leadership behavior influences adoption
  • Give champions time for peer support and measure whether adoption and task outcomes improve
  • Continuous education is required
  • Analytics should drive optimization
  • Business outcomes matter more than usage metrics
