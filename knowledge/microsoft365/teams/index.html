Microsoft Teams 거버넌스

Executive Summary​

Microsoft Teams is the primary collaboration platform within Microsoft 365.

However, many organizations experience rapid Teams sprawl, inconsistent ownership, excessive guest access and poor lifecycle management.

A successful Teams deployment requires governance across:

  • Team Provisioning
  • Ownership
  • Membership
  • Guest Access
  • External Collaboration
  • Information Protection
  • Information Barriers
  • Lifecycle Management
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot

The objective is to balance collaboration agility with security and operational control.

Business Scenario​

Common customer challenges:

Uncontrolled Team Creation

Guest Access Risk

Scenario 3​

Information Governance

Examples:

Segment-based communication restriction

Examples:

  • regulated departments that must not start 1:1 chat with each other

Teams Architecture​

Logical Architecture​

Microsoft 365 Group
  +-- Microsoft Teams
  +-- SharePoint team site
  +-- Exchange group mailbox
  +-- Shared OneNote notebook

A team can create a new Microsoft 365 group or use an existing group owned by its creator. These are related services, not a serial processing pipeline. Standard-channel files use the parent team's SharePoint site; private and shared channels each have a separate SharePoint site with their own membership boundary. See Microsoft 365 Groups and Teams and Teams/SharePoint integration.

Guest Access Framework​

Recommended Controls​

Guest Users:

  • MFA Required
  • Access Review
  • Time-bound guest access through an approved lifecycle process

Microsoft 365 group expiration renews or deletes the group; it is not an expiry date for each guest. For access granted through an access package, configure assignment expiration and review other access paths separately. See group expiration and external-user entitlement management.

Channel Strategy​

Standard Channels​

Visible to all team members.

Recommended for most use cases.

Private Channels​

Restricted membership.

Recommended for:

  • HR
  • Finance
  • Executive Content

Shared Channels​

Cross-team collaboration. External participation uses Microsoft Entra B2B direct connect, not a guest object in the host tenant. Both organizations must configure the required cross-tenant access and Teams settings. Teams guest access must also be enabled to invite external participants, even though the channel does not use guest accounts. Existing guests cannot simply be added to a shared channel. See shared channel prerequisites and limitations.

Recommended for:

  • Partner collaboration
  • Program management

Best Practice​

  • Require two owners per Team
  • Govern Team creation
  • Review guest access regularly
  • Archive inactive Teams
  • Align Teams governance with SharePoint governance
  • Review Teams before Copilot deployment
  • Use sensitivity labels consistently

Troubleshooting​

IssuePossible Cause to InvestigateCheck or Remediation
Too many TeamsNo creation governanceImplement approval workflow
Ownerless TeamsOwnership not enforcedAssign secondary owners
Guest sprawlNo guest review processImplement access reviews
Copilot poor responsesPoor content structureImprove information architecture
Security concernsWeak governanceApply sensitivity labels and DLP

Lessons Learned​

  • Define ownership and lifecycle tasks alongside Teams deployment
  • Ownerless Teams become operational risk
  • Guest access must be reviewed continuously
  • SharePoint governance and Teams governance must align
  • Copilot success depends on content quality
  • Track inactive workspaces, owner changes and unresolved access reviews over time

캐나다 시간대 변경과 Teams Rooms​

캐나다 일부 지역과 회의를 운영한다면 2026년 11월 1일 이후 일정을 점검하세요. Microsoft는 Windows용 Teams Rooms에서 Windows보다 시간이 한 시간 늦게 표시되는 문제에 대해 Edge·WebView2 업데이트 후 회의실 화면의 시간 확인을 안내합니다. 일반 Teams 클라이언트의 일정·알림과 다른 Teams 기기의 영향은 별도로 시험해야 합니다.

11월 2일 추가 안내에 따라 Teams Events의 임시 시간대는 Alberta가 (UTC-06:00) Saskatchewan, British Columbia가 (UTC-07:00) Yukon, Manitoba가 (UTC-05:00) Eastern Time (US & Canada)입니다. 이벤트 주최자가 선택한 값과 참석자 화면의 시간을 함께 확인하세요. Auto Attendant는 공식 안내에 연결된 설정 절차에 따라 시간대를 수동 변경해야 합니다. Shifts는 영향 조사 중이므로 관리자는 메시지 센터의 MC1532245를 확인하세요.

Microsoft의 제품별 시간대 변경 안내와 Outlook·Exchange Online 일정 점검을 참고하세요.
