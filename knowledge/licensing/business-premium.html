Microsoft 365 Business Premium

BUSINESS PREMIUM DECISION GUIDE

Use Business Premium when productivity and baseline security must move together

Business Premium is strongest when Microsoft 365 Apps, Entra ID, Intune and Defender for Business are planned as one SMB security baseline.

Executive Summary​

Microsoft 365 Business Premium is a strong option for small and mid-sized organizations that need productivity, identity protection, endpoint management and baseline security in one package.

It is often suitable when the organization wants Microsoft 365, Intune, Defender for Business and Entra ID capabilities without moving immediately to enterprise E3, E5 or E7 licensing.

한국어 요약​

Microsoft 365 Business Premium에는 업무 앱과 메일·파일 공유, Entra ID P1, Intune, Defender for Business가 포함됩니다. Teams는 구매한 상품의 포함 여부를 확인하세요.

메일과 Office 외에 MFA, Conditional Access, 기기 관리와 엔드포인트 보호가 필요한 조직에서 검토할 수 있습니다. 포함 기능과 구매할 상품의 Teams 제공 여부는 별도로 확인하세요.

필요한 보안·관리·규정 준수 기능이 Business Premium에 포함되는지 확인하고 부족한 기능은 추가 구독과 비교하세요.

Business Scenario​

  • Modernize email and collaboration
  • Manage Windows and mobile devices
  • Establish MFA and Conditional Access
  • Improve endpoint security
  • Standardize cloud productivity for growing teams

Architecture​

Business Premium capability pathProductivity to security baseline
01Users and appsProvide Microsoft 365 Apps, Exchange, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive for daily work.
02Identity baselineApply Entra ID, MFA, Conditional Access and account protection policies.
03Device managementUse Intune for device compliance, configuration and mobile application management.
04Endpoint securityDefender for Business adds endpoint protection, alerts and security operations baseline.

Implementation​

  1. Confirm user count and license eligibility.
  2. Enable MFA and identity baseline.
  3. Configure Intune enrollment and compliance policy.
  4. Deploy Microsoft 365 Apps.
  5. Enable Defender for Business.
  6. Review external sharing and data protection settings.

Licensing​

Microsoft documents a combined limit of 300 provisioned seats across Business Basic, Business Standard and Business Premium. Count all Business-family seats together; enterprise subscriptions are a separate option. Verify the purchased variant's Teams entitlement and any server add-ons in the Business Premium FAQ. 고급 규정 준수·보안 조사·통화 기능 등이 필요하면 E3·E5와 필요한 추가 기능을 비교합니다. 업무용 Copilot·Entra Suite·Agent 365까지 함께 필요하면 E7도 비교하세요.

When Business Premium Is Enough​

Business Premium is usually a practical fit when the organization needs:

  • Microsoft 365 productivity and collaboration as the main platform
  • baseline identity security with MFA and Conditional Access
  • Intune-based device enrollment, compliance policy and app protection
  • endpoint protection through Defender for Business
  • 실제로 운영할 수 있는 기기·계정·보안 관리 범위
  • a cost-conscious license path for a controlled Microsoft 365 rollout

E3·E5·E7과 비교할 때​

인원수만으로 상위 플랜을 정하지 말고, Business Premium과 추가 구독으로 필요한 기능을 충족할 수 있는지 확인하세요. E7에는 E5의 기능과 Microsoft 365 Copilot·Microsoft Entra Suite·Microsoft Agent 365가 포함됩니다. Microsoft 공식 비교와 E3·E5·E7 비교에서 포함 기능과 비용 조건을 확인하세요.

Security​

  • 일반 사용자에게 MFA를 적용하고 긴급 계정은 별도로 보호합니다.
  • Use Conditional Access templates carefully.
  • Manage devices through Intune.
  • Enable endpoint protection and security baselines.
  • Review admin roles and emergency access accounts.

긴급 액세스 계정은 일반 사용자의 차단·제한 조건부 액세스 정책에서 제외합니다. MFA를 없애는 대신 평소 관리자와 다른 피싱 방지 인증 수단을 준비하고 로그인 경보와 정기 접속 시험을 운영하세요. Microsoft 긴급 액세스 계정 지침을 따릅니다.

Decision Checklist​

QuestionRecommended Review
User count and license eligibilityConfirm Microsoft licensing boundaries before designing the final bill of materials.
Identity readinessCheck MFA, admin roles, emergency access, guest access and Conditional Access requirements.
Device management scopeConfirm Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and BYOD policy expectations.
Security baselineDecide which Microsoft security baseline, Defender configuration and alert ownership model will be used.
Data protectionReview external sharing, sensitivity, retention and DLP requirements before assuming Business Premium is enough.
Operations modelAssign ownership for user onboarding, device compliance, security alerts and policy exceptions.

Field Reference Pattern​

In retail, manufacturing and professional service environments, Business Premium is often used as a pragmatic modernization step: replace fragmented email and endpoint tools, standardize Microsoft 365 collaboration, enable MFA, and introduce Intune/Defender controls without creating an enterprise-scale program on day one.

The strongest results come when licensing, security baseline, device policy and operations handover are designed together.

Lessons Learned​

Business Premium can deliver strong value when implemented as a full security and management platform. If it is used only for email and Office apps, much of the package value is left unused.
