Request Guidance​

When requesting an editable document, include:

  • target workload such as Microsoft 365, Security, Copilot, Azure or Migration
  • customer scenario or industry without exposing confidential customer names
  • proposal or delivery phase
  • preferred document type such as SOW, WBS, assessment workbook or risk register
  • expected audience such as executive, technical team or project manager
