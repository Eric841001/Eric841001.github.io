Exchange Online OMEv2·RMS 첨부파일 암호 해제 규칙

EXCHANGE ONLINE ENCRYPTION EXCEPTION

Handle OMEv2 and RMS removal as a controlled exception

Transport rules that remove encryption must be narrow, approved, auditable and tied to a clear business process because they can increase data leakage risk.

ScopeNarrow
RuleEXO
RiskData
AuditEvidence

Executive Summary​

This guide describes how Exchange Online transport rules can be used to remove OMEv2 protection and RMS attachment encryption for specific outbound mail scenarios.

This configuration should be used carefully because it can remove encryption protection from sensitive documents.

Business Scenario​

Organizations may need to remove encryption from specific outbound messages when:

  • A trusted external recipient cannot open protected files
  • A business process requires recipient-side labeling
  • Inter-company collaboration requires file reclassification
  • A controlled exception is approved by security or compliance

Important Warning​

Removing encryption from email or attachments can increase data leakage risk.

This should only be used with:

  • Approved recipients
  • Narrow sender/recipient scope
  • Legal or compliance approval
  • Audit review
  • Periodic policy review

Architecture​

Encryption exception flowNarrow transport rule scope
01Internal senderApproved sender or business process requires a controlled encryption exception.
02Exchange OnlineMessage is evaluated by mail flow and transport rule conditions.
03Transport ruleConfigure removal of supported message or attachment encryption and test the rule against approved and mixed-recipient cases.
04External recipientVerify recipient access, retained protections and the exception record before approving rollout.

Control Scope​

ControlRecommendation
Recipient ScopeLimit to approved recipients or domains
Sender ScopeRestrict to internal users or specific groups
Rule ConditionUse clear and auditable conditions
Rule ActionRemove OMEv2 and RMS attachment encryption
ReviewReview periodically with security team

Example PowerShell Pattern​

RemoveOMEv2 removes Purview Message Encryption from the message and its attachments. RemoveRMSAttachmentEncryption also handles attachments encrypted before they were added, and requires RemoveOMEv2 $true. These settings are not a general decryptor for every protection format. Use the parameter definitions and validate the actual message and file protection types before approving an exception.

This removal applies to protection applied by your organization, including supported replies to messages your organization encrypted. It does not authorize decryption of protection owned by another organization. See mail flow encryption and removal scope.

Install the ExchangeOnlineManagement module before running the connection commands. The signed-in account needs the Exchange RBAC permissions for the rule commands and parameters. See Exchange Online PowerShell connection requirements.

Import-Module ExchangeOnlineManagement
Connect-ExchangeOnline

Create a transport rule for a specific approved recipient scenario. The SentTo value must resolve to a mailbox, mail user or mail contact in your Exchange organization; an arbitrary external SMTP address is not a directory recipient. For this example, first confirm the approved external recipient exists as a mail contact and use its identity. See mail flow rule recipient conditions. Test a message that also contains an unapproved recipient before rollout; do not assume recipient matching safely confines a message-level decryption action in every mixed-recipient case.

New-TransportRule `
  -Name "Remove OMEv2 for Approved External Recipient" `
  -SentTo "<approved-mail-contact-identity>" `
  -FromScope InOrganization `
  -RemoveOMEv2 $true

Enable RMS attachment encryption removal.

Set-TransportRule `
  "Remove OMEv2 for Approved External Recipient" `
  -RemoveRMSAttachmentEncryption $true

Validation​

Validate with test messages:

TestExpected Result
Approved recipientVerify removal for the supported protection type and successful recipient access
Non-approved recipientVerify protection remains intact, including messages with both approved and unapproved recipients
Internal recipientRule behavior follows defined condition
Audit reviewReview rule matches and independently confirm the received message and attachment protection

EAC의 Exchange Transport Rule 보고서에서 규칙 일치 건수를 확인할 수 있습니다. 규칙의 Severity가 Not audit이면 보고서에 포함되지 않습니다. 대부분의 기록은 24시간 안에 나타나지만 최대 5일이 걸릴 수 있습니다. 규칙이 일치했다는 기록만으로 첨부 파일의 암호화 해제까지 성공했다고 판단하지 말고, 시험 수신자가 받은 메일과 파일을 직접 확인하세요. 규칙 사용 현황 확인

한국어 요약​

Exchange Online 메일 흐름 규칙으로 승인된 예외 메일과 첨부파일의 암호화를 해제하는 방법을 설명합니다.

수신자·발신자·도메인을 좁게 지정하고 업무상 이유, 승인자와 예외 종료일을 기록하세요. 규칙 적용 후에는 승인 대상과 그 밖의 수신자를 각각 시험해야 합니다.
