Microsoft 365 진단표

Executive Summary​

This workbook provides a structured framework for assessing Microsoft 365 environments across identity, collaboration, security, compliance, governance and Copilot readiness.

The objective is to identify risks, optimization opportunities and strategic recommendations that support business transformation and cloud modernization.

Executive lens: Use this workbook to move from raw tenant observations to a decision-ready assessment. Scope, evidence, maturity score, risk, recommendation and roadmap should stay connected.

Assessment Workbook

Turn tenant observations into a decision-ready roadmap

The workbook connects scope, tenant evidence, maturity scoring, risks, recommendations and executive roadmap into one reusable assessment structure.

Overall Readiness​

For this author-proposed scorecard, score each assessed domain from 1 to 5, then calculate (average domain score - 1) / 4 × 100. Round to the nearest whole number before applying the bands below. Mark missing evidence as not assessed and report coverage separately; do not treat a partial score as permission to deploy. The bands are planning examples, rather than a Microsoft readiness certification.

ScoreReadiness Level
0-40Not Ready
41-60Partially Ready
61-80Ready with Improvements
81-100Enterprise Ready
