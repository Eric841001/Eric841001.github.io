아키텍처 빌더

Executive Summary​

This architecture builder provides a standardized framework for designing Microsoft 365, Azure, Security and Copilot architectures.

Use the framework to record design decisions and define tests for consistency, capacity, security and operations.

Enterprise Architecture Builder

Convert requirements into target architecture and delivery roadmap

Architecture Builder standardizes business requirements, current state, gap analysis, target design, governance, risk and operational handover across Microsoft cloud engagements.
BusinessCurrentTargetOperate
Architecture Design ModelRequirement to approved roadmap
01BusinessCapture drivers, constraints, stakeholders, compliance and transformation goals.02FoundationDesign identity, collaboration, security, governance and Copilot foundations.03TransitionDefine source, target, migration strategy, cutover and hypercare model.04OperateAssign owners, cadence, monitoring, handover and architecture review loop.
Architecture ruleDo not draw target architecture without decisions, risks, owners, roadmap and operating model.

Architecture Design Methodology​

Architecture Design MethodologyBusiness requirement to operational architecture
01RequirementsGoals, constraints, stakeholders, compliance and business outcomes.
02Current stateTenant, identity, workloads, security posture, governance and risks.
03Gap analysisControl gaps, dependencies, licensing needs and operational blockers.
04Target designMicrosoft 365, Azure, Security, Copilot and Migration architecture decisions.
05RoadmapImplementation phases, workstreams, milestones, owners and handover cadence.
