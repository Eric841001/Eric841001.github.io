## Sharing Policy

<div class="kc-maturity-ladder" aria-label="Downloads Center sharing policy">
  <div class="kc-maturity-step"><small>Public</small><strong>Methodology overview</strong><span>Available on the site as architecture guidance, decision logic and reusable consulting patterns.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-maturity-step"><small>Public</small><strong>Template structure</strong><span>Available as public-safe outlines showing sections, purpose and usage model.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-maturity-step"><small>Request</small><strong>Editable DOCX / XLSX / PPTX</strong><span>Shared when appropriate after scenario, audience and confidentiality review.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-maturity-step"><small>Sanitized</small><strong>Customer-specific deliverables</strong><span>Shared only as sanitized examples when sensitive details are removed.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-maturity-step"><small>Restricted</small><strong>Commercial or named references</strong><span>Pricing, discounts, contract terms and named references are not published publicly.</span></div>
</div>

## Confidentiality Guardrails

<div class="kc-platform-fit" aria-label="Downloads Center confidentiality guardrails">
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Identity</small><strong>Remove customer identity</strong><span>Customer names, tenant names, project code names and named references are not published.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Architecture</small><strong>Remove sensitive architecture details</strong><span>Tenant IDs, domains, IP ranges, policy IDs, exceptions and internal diagrams are removed.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Commercial</small><strong>Remove commercial information</strong><span>Pricing, discounts, contract terms and internal cost assumptions are not posted publicly.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Editable Files</small><strong>Share after context review</strong><span>DOCX, XLSX and PPTX versions are shared only after confirming intended use.</span></div>
</div>

## Quality Standard

<div class="kc-platform-fit" aria-label="Consulting asset quality standard">
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Context</small><strong>Business context and target audience</strong><span>Every asset should make clear who uses it and which decision or delivery activity it supports.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Scope</small><strong>Scope and out-of-scope boundary</strong><span>Strong templates prevent ambiguity by separating included work, exclusions and assumptions.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Inputs</small><strong>Required inputs and evidence</strong><span>Assessment and planning assets should define required data, owners, reviewers and evidence.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Decision</small><strong>Acceptance or decision criteria</strong><span>Proposal and delivery assets should show how decisions are made and accepted.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Governance</small><strong>Owner and approval model</strong><span>Assets should include owner, reviewer, approval, risk and follow-up sections.</span></div>
  <div class="kc-platform-fit__card"><small>Safety</small><strong>Public-safe and editable separation</strong><span>Public-safe samples and customer-specific editable versions must remain clearly separated.</span></div>
</div>

## 한국어 요약

Downloads Center는 실제 컨설팅에서 사용할 수 있는 Microsoft 365, Security, Copilot, AI Agent, Migration, SOW, WBS, Risk Register 관련 자산의 구조와 활용 방식을 설명하는 공간입니다. 실제 편집 가능한 문서는 공개 사이트에 직접 올리지 않고, 목적과 보안 경계를 확인한 뒤 공유하는 방식이 적합합니다.

첫 요청에서는 고객명, tenant ID, domain, 계약 금액, 내부 프로젝트명, 보안 예외 정보처럼 민감한 내용은 제외하고, 업종, 대상 workload, 프로젝트 단계, 필요한 산출물 유형만 전달하는 것이 안전합니다.
